# Voice and positions

## Audience
Complete beginners in home kitchens (default assumption; edit). They've probably seen sourdough on social media and may already have "failed a starter" once.

## The reader's promise
"By the end of this book you'll have a living starter, a schedule that fits your week, and a loaf you're proud of, and you'll know *why* each step works, so you can fix things yourself."
