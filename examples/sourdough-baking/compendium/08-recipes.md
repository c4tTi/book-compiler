# 8. Beginner formulas and recipe families

These formulas are synthesised from the sources; they're starting points for the author to test, not copied recipes.

## 8.1 Core beginner loaf (computed baker's percentages)
| Ingredient | Grams | Baker's % (total-flour basis) |
|---|---|---|
| Bread flour | 450 | — |
| Whole wheat flour | 50 | — |
| Water | 350 | — |
| Salt | 10 | 1.8% |
| Levain (100% hydration) | 100 | 9.1% prefermented flour |
| **Totals** | 960 g dough | **flour 550 g, water 400 g → 72.7% hydration** |

This sits squarely in the consensus beginner zone of 70–75% hydration, ~2% salt, ~10–20% prefermented flour [@leo_hydration; @ka_salt; @forkish2012; @robertson2010]. 🔎 Test-bake it in your own kitchen before publishing.

## 8.2 Recipe families to include
- **Country loaf / pain au levain:** the Tartine / FWSY archetype [@robertson2010; @forkish2012; @leo2022; @hamelman2004].
- **Whole-wheat and whole-grain loaves:** raise hydration by 10–15 points [@leo_hydration; @forkish2012].
- **Soft sandwich loaf in a tin:** a less intimidating first bake, and proof that sourdough isn't only open-crumb boules [@ka_myths; @reinhart2001].
- **Rye:** Danish rugbrød as an approachable, forgiving rye loaf (no shaping skill needed) [@ryebaker_danish; @hamelman2004].
- **Discard recipes:** pancakes, crackers, flatbreads; they also reduce waste [@foodprint_discard; @leo_weekend].
- **Gluten-free sourdough:** starters from GF flours are possible (Coeliac UK publishes a recipe for members). Needs dedicated equipment and ingredients [@coeliacuk_gf; @nakov2025].
- **Sweet enriched (panettone):** advanced; mention as aspiration only [@lievito_wordloaf].

## 8.3 Recipe sources worth mining (for testing and comparison)
- English-language: Forkish, Robertson, Leo, Hamelman, Reinhart, Whitley's *Do Sourdough*, Kimbell's *Sourdough School*, Raffa, Boddy, Hadjiandreou, Söderin (Swedish) [@forkish2012; @robertson2010; @leo2022; @hamelman2004; @reinhart2001; @whitley2014; @kimbell2018; @raffa2017; @boddy2022; @hadjiandreou2016; @soderin2014].
- Historic/US home: Ed Wood's *Classic Sourdoughs* (starters collected worldwide) and 1970s cookbooks [@wood2001; @wilford1971].
- German: Lutz Geißler [@geissler2017].

## Purpose note
- **Essential:** one master formula taught in full, with 3–4 variations (whole wheat, sandwich tin, rye, discard).
- **Optional:** GF and enriched breads.
- **Cut:** pizza, bagels and the like, unless the book widens scope. Remember that recipes themselves aren't copyrightable but their text and photos are, so write your own versions.
