## Calculators and apps
- Baker's-percentage and hydration calculators: Foodgeek [@foodgeek], GigaCalculator [@gigacalc], Flourwise app [@flourwise], iOS Sourdough Hydration Calc [@hydrationcalc_app]. Water-temperature (DDT) calculator from The Perfect Loaf [@leo_watercalc].
- ⚠️ All free tools but with varied quality. Check that a calculator counts the starter's flour and water. Many don't by default [@leo_hydration].

## Purpose note
- **Essential:** a minimal kit list (scale, jar, bowl, pot). Beginners overspend.
- **Optional:** calculator recommendations, as they date fast.
- **Cut:** brand comparisons.
