# 7. Troubleshooting: reading the loaf

## 7.1 Crumb diagnosis
| Symptom | Likely cause(s) | Also consider |
|---|---|---|
| Dense, tight, gummy between big holes; pale thick crust; "flying crust" | Under-fermented bulk / under-proofed [@cucuzza_crumb; @freshloaf_crumb] | Weak starter, low-protein flour, <70% hydration, under-baked (check ~96°C/205°F) [@cucuzza_crumb; @leo_dutch] |
| Flat, spreading, ragged irregular holes, weak pale crust, sour/boozy | Over-fermented (usually in bulk) [@cucuzza_crumb; @cucuzza_crumbpdf] | Too-high hydration for the flour, weak shaping [@se_flatloaf] |
| Sticky, unmanageable dough | Hydration too high for flour; under-developed gluten | Lower water 5%, add folds [@leo_hydration; @se_flatloaf] |
| Too sour / not sour enough | See 7.2 | |
| Bottom burnt | Pot too hot / too long | Sheet pan under Dutch oven, lower temp [@leo_dutch] |

- ✅ Symptoms overlap, so diagnose from several clues. The Sourdough Journey's crumb guide (20+ photographed loaves) is the best visual reference found [@cucuzza_crumbpdf; @sourdoughjourney_yt].

## 7.2 Controlling sourness
From Leo's levers [@leo_sour] (consistent with King Arthur and The Sourdough Framework [@ka_myths; @kleinwachter2022]):

| More sour | Less sour |
|---|---|
| Use starter past peak | Use starter at peak / young levain (3–4 h) |
| Stiff levain (50–65% hydration), which favours acetic acid | Liquid levain (100%), which favours milder lactic acid |
| More whole grain, especially rye (buffering) | Refined white flour |
| Warm (78–82°F) fermentation | Cooler, shorter bulk and proof |
| Long cold retard (24–48 h) | Short or no retard |
| Small levain (10–15% prefermented flour) | Larger levain (25–35%) |

- ⚠️ The temperature–acid relationship is often oversimplified ("cold = more acetic"). Warmer temperatures give more *total* acid; cooler conditions shift the *ratio* toward acetic but produce less acid overall [@leo_sour]. Say it carefully.

## 7.3 Starter problems
- No rise after a week: keep going, use whole grain/rye, keep it warm (24–27°C), consider the juice method [@freshloaf_starter; @se_notrising; @ka_guide].
- Hooch, strange smells (acetone, cheese): hunger signals; feed more often or at higher ratios [@pantrymama_mold; @ka_myths].
- Mould / pink / orange: discard (ch. 10) [@pantrymama_mold].
