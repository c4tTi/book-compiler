# 13. Learning resources and community

## 13.1 Books (for the author's comparison shelf)
| Book | Role | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Forkish, *Flour Water Salt Yeast* (2012) | Most-recommended beginner book | Prescriptive schedules [@forkish2012; @cucuzza_books] |
| Robertson, *Tartine Bread* (2010) | Aspirational classic | Intuitive, less prescriptive [@robertson2010] |
| Leo, *The Perfect Loaf* (2022) | Modern all-rounder | James Beard 2023, QR videos [@leo2022] |
| Hamelman, *Bread* (2004/2021) | Professional reference | Levain types, rye [@hamelman2004] |
| Reinhart, *Bread Baker's Apprentice* (2001) | Classic teaching book | [@reinhart2001] |
| Kleinwächter, *The Sourdough Framework* (2022) | Free, open, science-led | CC BY-SA, so quotable with attribution [@kleinwachter2022] |
| Whitley, *Do Sourdough* / *Bread Matters* | UK campaigner-baker | [@whitley2014; @whitley2006] |
| Kimbell, *The Sourdough School* | Health framing | [@kimbell2018] |
| Gobbetti & Gänzle, *Handbook on Sourdough Biotechnology* | Academic | [@gobbetti2012] |

- ⚠️ "Best book" lists online are mostly affiliate-driven. Forkish and Tartine appear in all of them [@cucuzza_books].

## 13.2 Video
- Bake with Jack, "101 Beginners No-Knead Sourdough Loaf" [@bakewithjack]; The Bread Code [@breadcode_yt]; The Sourdough Journey [@sourdoughjourney_yt]; Foodgeek [@foodgeek]. An overview of the YouTube landscape: [@falstaff_yt].

## 13.3 Audio
- Gastropod [@gastropod2017]; Science Friday [@scifri_microbiome]; The Sourdough Podcast [@buehler_podcast]; Scientific American [@sciam_antifungal].

## 13.4 Courses
- San Francisco Baking Institute online library (240+ videos) and in-person "Sourdough at Home" [@sfbi_online]; local classes, e.g. Josey Baker Bread [@joseybaker_class]; King Arthur's school lesson plan [@ka_bfs_lesson].

## 13.5 Communities
- The Fresh Loaf forum and handbook [@freshloaf_forum; @freshloaf_starter]; r/Sourdough [@reddit_sourdough]; Stack Exchange Seasoned Advice [@se_growyeast]; Hacker News threads, where tech-minded bakers discuss The Sourdough Framework [@hn_makeyourown; @hn_framework].
