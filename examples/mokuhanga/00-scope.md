# Scope: Mokuhanga (Japanese Woodblock Printing)

- **Request:** I want to teach a weekend workshop on Japanese woodblock printing (mokuhanga). Pull together everything you can find on it, history, techniques, materials, where to buy tools, the lot.
- **Purpose:** Teaching a weekend workshop
- **Audience:** The user (workshop leader, assumed to have some printmaking experience) and their workshop participants: adult beginners, mixed backgrounds, ~8-12 people
- **Depth:** Enough history and context to give a 30-45 min talk; practical depth on materials, tools, techniques and suppliers to run two days of hands-on printing; pointers for going deeper
- **Languages / regions:** English primarily; Japanese-language sources and suppliers where findable (topic has Japanese roots); suppliers in US, UK/EU and Japan
- **Out of scope:** Western oil-based relief/woodcut and linocut (only as contrast); art-market investing/valuation of antique prints; deep connoisseurship of individual ukiyo-e artists
- **Started:** 2026-09-28

This file is a working document: edit or rewrite any part of it freely.

## Coverage notes (why some lenses are thin)

- **critical:** Mokuhanga has no organized skeptic literature. "Critical" here means safety guidance, the scholarly critique of shin-hanga exoticism, the reproductions/fakes literature, and accounts of craft decline. Searches for an explicit critique of Western mokuhanga (appropriation, dilution) found none. Recorded as a gap, not as consensus.
- **official:** No regulator governs the craft. Official voices are museums, Japan's Agency for Cultural Affairs (Selected Conservation Technique), the Tokyo craft designation, UNESCO (washi) and university EHS offices.
- **data:** Relevant only as image databases (ukiyo-e.org, ARC). There are no statistics on practitioners or the market.
- **community:** Reddit and Facebook groups were not reachable. Baren Forum, the IMA and the podcast stand in.
- **Types absent:** standard, app, software, person. None are meaningful for this topic.
