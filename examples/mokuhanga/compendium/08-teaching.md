✅ **The pattern is consistent:** day 1 is design, transfer, kento and carving; day 2 is paper preparation and printing. The realistic target is **a small two-block (or two-colour) print in a small edition** [@highpoint-2025; @vollmer-goldenroad-video].

## 8.2 What educators say

- ✅ The 2017 International Mokuhanga Conference had a cluster of teaching papers [@imc-2017-papers]:
  - Jennifer Mack-Watkins, *Promoting and Passing on the Tradition of Mokuhanga*, which gives a **format for planning a mokuhanga workshop for larger audiences**. It's the closest thing to a published model for your task; 🔎 find the full paper.
  - John Amoss, on balancing tradition and technology with university students.
  - Melissa Schulenberg, on teaching non-art-majors.
  - Paola González, on adopting mokuhanga in schools and communities in Chile as a non-toxic method.
- ✅ Foncerrada Moreno's qualitative study (Spain) finds that bringing mokuhanga into higher education encourages interdisciplinary experimentation and revitalises printmaking. It is based on interviews and has no controlled outcomes, so treat it as supportive rather than proof ⚠️ [@moreno2026].
- ✅ The Spanish-, Turkish- and Portuguese-language literature shows the method being taught well beyond Japan and the Anglosphere [@gilalares2023; @gilalares-review2024; @karaalan2020; @shimizu2018].
- ✅ Podcasts with teachers discuss adapting mokuhanga for students (Kate MacDonagh, Terry McKenna's "It's for everybody") [@unfinished-print-macdonagh; @unfinished-print-mckenna; @unfinished-print].

## 8.3 Weekend-specific lessons (synthesis)

1. ✅ **Pre-prepare what isn't the lesson.** Sharpen tools; pre-cut blocks to size; buy pre-sized paper; maybe pre-cut kento on the keyblock [@highpoint-2025; @mckenna-sharp-tools; @hiromi-sized-papers] 🔎 (the last two are common choices but not documented in a source read here).
2. ✅ **Design small and simple:** postcard to A5, two blocks, big shapes plus one line block [@highpoint-2025; @vollmer-goldenroad-video].
3. ✅ **Carving takes longer than people expect.** Nam sends carving home. For a two-day weekend, give Saturday afternoon plus Sunday morning to carving, or supply a pre-carved "practice block" so participants can learn printing while they carve [@highpoint-2025; @tokyoweekender-revival] (Mokuhankan's "print parties" use pre-cut blocks for beginners).
4. ✅ **Make the damp pack Saturday evening** so it's ready Sunday [@highpoint-2025].
5. ✅ **Teach printing on a proofing block first**, so the pigment/paste/water balance and baren pressure are learned before the real edition [@rospo-errors].
6. ✅ **Normalise failure.** Hand out the troubleshooting table (chapter 5) and let participants pull 10–20 sheets, knowing that a third will be proofs [@rospo-errors].
7. ✅ **Give a path onward:** a book (Vollmer, Salter or Boswell), an online course (McKenna), a community (Baren Forum, IMA), a supplier list [@vollmer2015; @salter2001; @boswell2019; @mokuhanga-school-online; @baren-forum; @ima-conferences].

## Purpose note

- **Essential:** the two-block goal, pre-prep, carving time budget, damp pack overnight, proofing block.
- **Optional:** find and read Mack-Watkins (2017) before designing your handout.
- **Cut:** more than one special effect; editions larger than about 10.
