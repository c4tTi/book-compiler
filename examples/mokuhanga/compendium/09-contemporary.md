# 9. Contemporary practice, community and debates

Legend: ✅ well established · ⚠️ contested or weakly supported · 🔎 verify before relying on it

## 9.1 The international network

- ✅ **International Mokuhanga Association (IMA)** runs conferences: 2017 (with a published papers list), **2024 in Echizen** ("Inheritance & Innovation: Mokuhanga Artists Explore Japanese Paper", 4–6 April 2024, with a papermaking focus and a juried show of 140 prints), and **2027 at the University of Kansas** [@ima-conferences; @imc-2017-papers; @imc-2024-echizen; @imc-2027-kansas].
- ✅ **MI-LAB** (Mokuhanga Innovation Laboratory) residencies near Lake Kawaguchi, with 2024 programmes near Echizen Washi Village. Five-week basic and advanced training by Japanese craftsmen [@milab-transartists; @airj-milab; @vollmer-milab].
- ✅ **Baren Forum**: a long-running online community with regular print exchanges (members make about 30 prints and receive a collated set). A nice idea for a follow-up to your workshop [@baren-forum; @mcclains-organizations].
- ✅ **The Unfinished Print** podcast (André Zadorozny) interviews dozens of practitioners [@unfinished-print; @unfinished-print-schofield; @unfinished-print-macdonagh; @unfinished-print-mckenna].
- ✅ Collectives and exhibitions: Mokuhanga Sisters; Honolulu Museum's *Enduring Impressions*; recent research-practice writing in *IMPACT Printmaking Journal* [@mokuhanga-sisters; @honolulu-enduring; @norman2025].
- ✅ Hacker News-style tech communities meet the topic through Mokuhankan's projects and print catalogues [@hn-hasui2025]. Popular coverage: Tokyo Weekender, tsunagu Japan, Making A Mark [@tokyoweekender-revival; @tsunagu-mokuhanga; @makingamark-2025].

## 9.2 In Japan

- ✅ Professional ukiyo-e craft survives in a few workshops (Adachi, the preservation society, Takezasadō, Mokuhankan). They make reproductions and commission contemporary artists to reach new buyers [@adachi-institute; @nippon-adachi; @choshugijutsu; @takezasado-tools; @mokuhankan; @ukiyoeinplay-about].
- ✅ **Succession:** the Adachi Foundation was created in 1994 because ageing and too few successors threatened the carving and printing skills. Training takes about five years to reach independence [@adachi-institute; @adachi-internship-ja; @nippon-adachi].

## 9.3 Debates and critical points (useful for discussion)

1. ⚠️ **Tool and material supply is fragile.** Only one or two professional hon-baren makers remain. Good hand-forged steel is harder to find. Tiny family businesses supply brushes, paper and sizing [@bull-gosho-baren; @wiki-baren; @bull-supply-sources; @ima-2024-dealers]. Western mokuhanga depends on this shrinking base, while Western demand also helps sustain it (IMC 2024 was held in a papermaking town with industry sponsors) [@imc-2024-echizen].
2. ⚠️ **"Non-toxic, eco-friendly".** The claim is broadly fair (no solvents, no press), but pigments still need SDS care, *dōsa* uses animal glue, and plywood uses adhesives [@uvm-printmaking-safety; @makingamark-2025; @tokyoweekender-revival]. Popular articles repeat the claim without caveats [@tokyoweekender-revival].
3. ⚠️ **Tradition vs. innovation / who owns the method.** International practitioners frame their work as exchange rather than appropriation. They adapt the method (plywood, watercolour, abstraction, mixed media) while crediting its Japanese origins [@moreno2026; @imc-2017-papers; @academia-contemporary; @petit1977; @wilhide2024]. Explicit critique of Western mokuhanga as appropriation was **not found** in this search. It's a gap, not evidence of consensus.
4. ⚠️ **Shin-hanga and exoticism**, and the Western appetite for a "timeless Japan" [@asianreview-shin-hanga; @wiki-shin-hanga] (chapter 1).
5. ⚠️ **Originals, reprints, reproductions, fakes.** A useful reality check for participants who collect [@ua-reproductions; @artelino-impressions].
6. ✅ **Science vs. folklore on colour.** Recent non-invasive analyses both confirm and correct traditional accounts (extensive Prussian blue in 19th-century prints; first analytical evidence of mica; organic dyes identified) [@heritage-kirazuri-2025; @paget2026; @biron2018; @villafana2019].
