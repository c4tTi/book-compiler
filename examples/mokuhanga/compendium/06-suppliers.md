✅ For a class of 8–12, the Cornelissen-style set is the best value because it covers carving and printing for one person. McClain's kit is a printing kit: it assumes you already have carving tools. Chapter 6.4 and the workbench budget sheet show the numbers.

## 6.2 Suppliers by region

### United States and Canada
- **McClain's Printmaking Supplies**: the widest Western range of Japanese tools (Futatsu-wari, Namisei, Josei, Aogami grades), plus plywood, papers, *dōsa* kit, kento how-to PDFs [@mcclains-woodblock-tools; @mcclains-kento-pdf; @mcclains-dosa-pdf; @mcclains-kit].
- **Hiromi Paper** (Culver City, CA): Japanese papers, including a "sized papers" range; Vollmer's book [@hiromi-sized-papers; @hiromi-kizuki-hosho].
- **Washi Arts**: paper, tools, Kean ball-bearing baren ($175–185) [@washiarts-kean-baren].
- **Paper Connection International** (RI), **The Japanese Paper Place** (Toronto): paper and barens [@vollmer-resources; @japanesepaperplace-baren].
- **Yasutomo**: nori paste [@yasutomo-nori].
- Supply lists from teachers: Woodstock School of Art, A. J. Marx, Mokuhanga Project Space [@woodstock-supply-list; @ajmarx-suppliers; @mps-resources].

### UK and Europe
- **Handprinted** (UK), prices at fetch: hangi-tō £31; U-gouge £32; plastic baren £6.90; coiled bamboo baren £50; Kean baren from £108; economy shina ply from £1.30; nori from £1.30; camellia oil £16.30 [@handprinted-japanese; @handprinted-tokyo].
- **Jackson's Art**: Japanese woodcut section; blog guides [@jacksons-japanese-supplies; @jacksons-blog-2019; @jacksons-holbein-2024].
- **L. Cornelissen & Son** (London): the kit above [@cornelissen-set].
- **Hickman Design**: imported kits and tools [@hickman-kit].
- Laura Boswell's supplier notes (UK artist who buys in Tokyo) [@boswell-suppliers].

### Japan (online or in person)
- The **International Mokuhanga Association's 2024 dealer list** gives about 35 shops with addresses. Highlights [@ima-2024-dealers]:
  - **Wood-Like Matsumura** (Nerima, Tokyo): "pretty much everything". Blocks, plywood, tools, barens, paper, pigments. Boswell's favourite; Bull recommends it [@bull-supply-sources; @boswell-suppliers].
  - **Michihamono** (Kita, Tokyo): carving tools, from school to professional grade, with an English site [@michihamono-hanga].
  - **Bumpodō** (Jimbōchō) / gazaiyasan.com online: tools, blocks, paper, pigments [@gazaiyasan-bumpodo].
  - **Sekaidō** (Shinjuku): general art store with ball-bearing barens [@boswell-suppliers].
  - **Gasendō** (Kyoto), **Morita Washi** (Kyoto), **Pigment Tokyo**, **Ozu Washi**, brush makers (Miyakawa, Kobayashi, Edoya) [@ima-2024-dealers; @pigment-tokyo-dosa].
  - **Kawasei Hamono** (Echigo Yoita): forged carving tools [@kawasei-tools-2021].
- **Karuizawa Mokuhanga School shop**: ships Japanese supplies worldwide [@mokuhanga-school-shop].
- **Mokuhankan** (Asakusa): mainly prints, plus advice and "print parties" [@mokuhankan; @boswell-suppliers; @tokyoweekender-revival].
- ⚠️ Bull's older advice on ordering from small Japanese makers still applies: they are tiny operations with little English, so be patient, and pay by card [@bull-supply-sources].

## 6.3 What to buy at what level

| Item | Workshop grade (per person) | "Next step" upgrade |
|---|---|---|
| Blocks | Shina plywood, about A5, two per person (+1 spare) | Better-grade shina; cherry for keyblocks |
| Tools | Beginner set (hangi-tō + aisuki + small U) | Laminated-steel hangi-tō from McClain's or Michihamono |
| Baren | Plastic or daiyō | Ball-bearing (Kean) or murasaki baren; hon-baren is for experts |
| Paper | Pre-sized machine-made or mid-grade hanga paper | Echizen hōsho / kizuki-hōsho |
| Colour | Tube watercolour or gouache + nori | Pigment dispersions (Holbein), sumi |
| Brushes | 2 small printing brushes | Horse-hair surikomi-bake in several sizes |

## Purpose note

- **Essential:** decide kit vs. assembling your own at least 4–6 weeks ahead (Japanese and US shipping takes time); order paper early and test it.
- **Hand-out:** one page listing 2–3 suppliers in the participants' country and 1–2 books.
- ⚠️ Prices, stock and exchange rates change. Treat the numbers here as ballpark.
