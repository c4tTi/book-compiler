# Weekend workshop syllabus: Introduction to Mokuhanga

A draft to adapt. It assumes 8–12 adult beginners, two days of about 6 hours each, and a teacher who has done at least a few mokuhanga prints. Readings refer to the registry by `[@id]` (details in `compendium/99-bibliography.md`). The structure follows the carve-then-print pattern of documented short courses [@highpoint-2025; @schofield-mokuhanga] (see compendium ch. 8).

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the weekend participants can:
1. Explain in two minutes what makes mokuhanga different from Western woodcut (water-based pigment + nori, damp washi, kento, baren, no press) and name its Edo and 20th-century roots.
2. Design a small image for **two blocks** (keyblock + one colour block) and transfer it to shina plywood.
3. Cut **kento** registration and carve lines with a **hangi-tō** and clear with an **aisuki**, working safely.
4. Prepare a **damp pack** and print a small registered edition (goal: 3–6 good prints out of 10–15 pulls) with a baren.
5. Diagnose common printing faults and know where to learn more and buy supplies.

## Before the weekend (teacher)

- [ ] Order kits and paper 4–6 weeks ahead (compendium ch. 6; `supply-budget.md`).
- [ ] Test **your** paper, pigments and nori together: wetting time, pigment:paste balance [@rospo-errors; @hiromi-sized-papers].
- [ ] Sharpen all tools [@mckenna-sharp-tools].
- [ ] Cut blocks to size (e.g. A5 / 148×210 mm) and paper to block size plus margin.
- [ ] Carve a **demo proofing block** per table (a simple shape plus a gradation area) for printing practice.
- [ ] Optional: pre-cut kento on the blocks to save 30–45 minutes (trade-off: participants lose a key skill).
- [ ] Print a pack of reference images [@ukiyoe-org; @arc-portal] and bring a few real prints or washi samples.
- [ ] SDS folder, first-aid kit, aprons, towels [@uvm-printmaking-safety].
- [ ] Email participants: "bring 2–3 simple drawings or photos, about postcard size, strong shapes".

## Day 2 (Sunday): Printing

| Time | Session | Activities | Readings / sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| 09:30–10:00 | **Finish carving** colour block | Clear borders thoroughly (prevents stray marks) | [@rospo-errors] |
| 10:00–10:45 | **Printing demo on proofing block** | Wet the block; nori + pigment; brush; lay paper into kagi then hikitsuke; baren; peel. Explain pigment:paste:water balance | [@tanuki-process; @japanesepaperplace-baren; @bm-hiroshige-blog] |
| 10:45–11:30 | **Practice printing** | Each person prints the proofing block 3–5 times; compare results | [@rospo-errors] |
| 11:30–11:45 | Break | | |
| 11:45–13:00 | **Print colour block** (light colour first) | Full run of 10–15 sheets; sheets go back into the damp pack | [@vollmer2015; @salter2001] |
| 13:00–13:45 | Lunch | | |

## Assessment (informal)

- A registered two-block print, with at least one sheet where both blocks align.
- Each participant can name one fault they hit and its fix.
- Optional: participants swap one print each, a mini Baren Forum-style exchange [@baren-forum].

## Risks and fallbacks

| Risk | Fallback |
|---|---|
| Carving runs over time | Pre-carved colour blocks; or print the keyblock only plus a hand-brushed bokashi |
| Paper too wet or too dry | Test beforehand; bring a spray bottle and dry newsprint to adjust |
| Registration fails | Print single-block images; use a jig (a board with an L-stop) as a crutch |
| Someone is cut | First-aid kit; pause the class for a safety reminder |
