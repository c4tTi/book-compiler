# Reading and viewing list

Three tiers for the teacher and for participants. IDs refer to `sources.jsonl` and `compendium/99-bibliography.md`.

## For you, before teaching (core)

| What | Why | ID |
|---|---|---|
| Vollmer, *Japanese Woodblock Print Workshop* (2015) | The standard English manual, with supplier and class appendices | [@vollmer2015] |
| McKenna, *Mokuhanga Fundamentals* (2020) + online course | Step-by-step for complete beginners; good model for teaching sequence | [@mckenna-fundamentals-book; @mokuhanga-school-online] |
| McClain's *How to Carve Kento* (PDF) | Precise, free, printable | [@mcclains-kento-pdf] |
| Stone, "Common moku hanga printing errors" | Your troubleshooting sheet | [@rospo-errors] |
| Nam's Highpoint two-session workshop description | A tested short-course structure | [@highpoint-2025] |
| IMC 2017 teaching papers (Mack-Watkins, Amoss, Schulenberg) | Published thinking on teaching mokuhanga | [@imc-2017-papers] |
| David Bull's encyclopedia: tools, baren, suppliers | Practical and frank | [@bull-carving-tools; @bull-alt-baren; @bull-supply-sources] |

## Recommended (history and context for the talk)

| What | Why | ID |
|---|---|---|
| Met Heilbrunn essay on ukiyo-e | Short, authoritative | [@met-ukiyoe-essay] |
| Korenberg, *The making and evolution of Hokusai's Great Wave* | Impressions, block wear, Prussian blue | [@korenberg-2020] |
| Merritt, *Modern Japanese Woodblock Prints: The Early Years* | Shin-hanga and sōsaku-hanga in depth | [@merritt-1990] |
| Tokuno 1894 (free on Internet Archive) | Primary 19th-c. account of the craft | [@tokuno-1894] |
| Yoshida Hiroshi 1939 | A master's own manual | [@yoshida-1939] |
| Salter 2001 | Concise alternative manual | [@salter2001] |

## Advanced / going deeper

- Laitinen, Moilanen & Tanttu (1999), the Finnish school [@laitinen1999]; Fletcher 1916 (free) [@fletcher1916]; Yoshida & Yuki 1966 [@yoshida-yuki-1966].
- Colour science: Villafana & Edwards 2019 (open access) [@villafana2019]; Paget 2026 [@paget2026]; Sasaki & Webber on dayflower [@dayflower-study].
- Residencies: MI-LAB [@milab-transartists]; the conferences [@imc-2024-echizen; @imc-2027-kansas].
- Non-English manuals: Gil Alares (Spanish) [@gilalares2023]; Japanese beginner books by Sasajima and Hayashi [@sasajima1984; @hayashi1988], Ono Tadashige's 1956 *Mokuhanga* [@ono1956], and the MOA Museum catalogue of modern Japanese prints [@moa1983] (catalogue records only, not read).
- Older English primers: Keiko Hiratsuka Moore's *Moku-Hanga* (1973) [@moore1973]; Boswell's book page with sample spreads [@boswell-mjwp-page].
- Quick popular histories (dealer or retailer sites, low weight): [@artelino-ukiyoe-to-mokuhanga; @hickman-history].
