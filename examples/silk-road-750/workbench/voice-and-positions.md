## Rules of the world (quick reference while drafting)

- Distances: Chang'an → Samarkand ≈ 6–12 months by caravan (`travel.py`).
- Money: silk bolts (40 *chi*) for big sums, strings of bronze cash for small ones; Sasanian silver is old-fashioned by 750.
- Paperwork: a *guosuo* permit lists every person and animal; changing route needs a new one.
- Cities lock their ward gates at night (Chang'an).
- Samarkand's ruler in 750: the ikhshid Turgar, under Abbasid Khurasan; Bukhara burned that year.
- Kucha: Tang HQ, Tocharian-speaking, Buddhist, musicians.
- Nobody says "Silk Road." The word for foreigners from the west is *hu*.
- Slavery is legal and common; slaves need a market certificate.
- After Dec 755, being visibly Sogdian in north China becomes dangerous.
