# Scope: The Silk Road c. 750 AD: Tang China to Samarkand

- **Request:** I'm writing a historical novel set on the Silk Road around 750 AD (Tang China to Samarkand). Gather every resource you can: history, daily life, trade goods, religions, languages, maps, primary sources, the lot.
- **Purpose:** writing a historical novel
- **Audience:** the novelist (author research), not the novel's readers; assumes an educated non-specialist
- **Depth:** research compendium deep enough to write scenes with confidence: political context, travel logistics, material detail, belief, speech and names, plus where to read primary sources in translation
- **Languages / regions:** Tang China (Chang'an, Hexi Corridor, Dunhuang), the Tarim Basin / Western Regions (Turfan, Kucha, Khotan, Kashgar), the Pamirs, Ferghana, Sogdiana (Samarkand, Panjikent, Bukhara). Sources in English first; Chinese, French, Russian, German, Japanese scholarship flagged where it is the main literature
- **Out of scope:** the Han-era Silk Road and the Mongol era except as background; the maritime routes except briefly; Rome/Byzantium except as the far western end. Time window roughly 700–790 AD, centred on 750 (Abbasid revolution 750, Talas 751, An Lushan rebellion 755–763)
- **Started:** 2026-09-28

This file is a working document: edit or rewrite any part of it freely.
