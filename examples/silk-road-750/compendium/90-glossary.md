# Glossary

| Term | Meaning | Chapter / source |
|---|---|---|
| Anxi (安西) | "Pacify the West": Tang protectorate general, seat at Kucha | 01, 08 [@wp-anxi] |
| Astana | Cemetery near Gaochang/Turfan, source of the Turfan documents | 03, 09 [@hansen2005] |
| Beiting (北庭) | Tang protectorate north of the Tianshan (from 702) | 01 [@wp-anxi] |
| Bolt (*pi* 匹) | Standard length of silk, 40 *chi*; hemp bolt (*duan*) 50 *chi*; used as money | 03 [@hansenrong2013] |
| Čākar | Sogdian retainer-soldier | 05, 08 [@spp306] |
| Chach | Tashkent | 08 [@wp-anxi] |
| Daqin (大秦) | Chinese name for the Roman world; "Luminous Religion of Daqin" = Church of the East | 05 [@xianstele] |
| Guosuo (過所) | Travel permit listing every person and animal; checked at passes | 02, 04 [@hansen2005; @emco-passport] |
| Hu (胡) | Western foreigners, especially Iranians/Sogdians | 06, 07 [@rong-newlight] |
| Hufu (胡服) | Foreign-style (riding) dress | 04 [@berman-hufu] |
| Huxuan (胡旋) | The Sogdian whirl dance | 04 [@wp-whirl] |
| Ikhshid | Title of the ruler of Samarkand | 01 [@wp-turgar] |
| Jiedushi (節度使) | Military governor commanding a frontier army | 01 [@wp-anlushan] |
| Jingjiao (景教) | "Luminous Religion": Church of the East Christianity | 05 [@xianstele] |
| Karluks | Turkic confederation; defected at Talas; later held Suyab | 01, 08 [@wp-talas; @wp-turgesh] |
| Li (里) | Chinese mile, roughly 0.5 km in the Tang 🔎 | 02 [@chiculture-posts] |
| Nana | Sogdian civic goddess seated on a lion | 05 [@wp-nana] |
| Ossuary | Box for cleaned bones in Sogdian burial | 05 [@spp306] |
| Sabao (薩保) | From Sogdian *sārtpāw*, "caravan leader": head of a Sogdian community in China | 03, 07 [@wp-sabao] |
| Sogdiana / Sughd | Zarafshan valley: Samarkand, Bukhara, Panjikent, Kesh | 07 [@vaissiere2005] |
| Tocharian B | Indo-European language of Kucha | 06 [@cetom] |
| Turgesh | Turkic khaganate (699–766) centred on Suyab | 01 [@wp-turgesh] |
| Xian (祆) | Chinese term for the Sogdian/Zoroastrian cult | 05 [@rong2014] |
| Xiyu (西域) | "Western Regions": Tarim Basin and beyond | 01 [@hansenrong2013] |
| Xizhou (西州) | Tang prefecture at Gaochang/Turfan after 640 | 02 [@hansen2005] |
| Zhaowu jiuxing (昭武九姓) | "Nine surnames of Zhaowu": Chinese name for the Sogdian city-states | 06 [@rong-newlight] |
| Zhejie (柘羯) | Rebel troops' name, from a Sogdian word for professional soldier ⚠️ | 08 [@si-sogdians] |
| Zuoren (作人) | Dependent labourer who, unlike a slave, could not be sold | 04 [@hansen2005] |
