# 7. Peoples and social types

Confidence: ✅ well established · ⚠️ contested or weakly supported · 🔎 verify before relying on it

This chapter is a cast list: the kinds of people who were actually on the route in 750, and the best sources for each.

## 7.1 Peoples

- **Sogdians.** Iranian-speaking city dwellers of the Zarafshan valley (Samarkand, Bukhara, Panjikent, Kesh) and a diaspora through the Tarim, Gansu, the Chinese capitals and the steppe ✅ [@vaissiere2005; @si-sogdians; @rong-newlight]. They were merchants, but also farmers, craftsmen, soldiers, interpreters and officials ✅ [@skaff2003]. Russian/Soviet archaeology at Panjikent (Marshak, Belenitsky) and Chinese tomb archaeology (Rong) are the two great evidence bases [@ru-sogdians; @marshak2002; @rong2014]. The Japanese synthesis by Moriyasu emphasises Sogdians and Uyghurs as the real engine of the "Tang" Silk Road ⚠️ (not verified in this session) [@moriyasu2016].
- **Tang Chinese.** Officials, garrison soldiers, convicts and settlers in the Western Regions; merchants increasingly in the 8th century ✅ [@hansen2005; @skaff2012].
- **Turks and Uyghurs.** Steppe peoples with their own imperial ideology and dealings with the Tang that mirrored Chinese practice ✅ [@skaff2012; @wp-orkhon; @wp-uyghur]. Karluks decided Talas ✅ [@wp-talas].
- **Tibetans.** The other empire in the Tarim ✅ [@beckwith1987].
- **Arabs and Persians.** Conquerors and settlers in Khurasan and Sogdiana; merchants in Chinese ports (massacred in Yangzhou, 760) ✅ [@wp-anlushan; @tabari27].
- **Tocharians and Khotanese.** Buddhist oasis populations of Kucha, Karashahr and Khotan ✅ [@spp085; @wp-khotan].
- **Koreans.** Gao Xianzhi (Goguryeo descent), the monk Hyecho, envoys on the Afrasiab mural ✅ [@wp-anxi; @hyecho; @wp-afrasiab].

## 7.3 Tensions between groups

- After the rebellion, Sogdians were targeted: at Fanyang people were "identified through their big noses" and killed; An Chongzhang changed his surname to Li in shame ✅ [@wp-anlushan]. Anti-*hu* sentiment was a real, datable shift.
- The Orkhon inscriptions warn Turks against Chinese ways ✅ [@wp-orkhon].
- In Sogdiana, tax and conversion pressure under the Abbasids fed revolt ✅ [@wp-sharik; @wp-transoxiana].

## Purpose note

- **Essential:** the Sogdian diaspora as the connective tissue; mixed-origin frontier armies; slaves and captives as travellers; the post-755 backlash against Sogdians.
- **Optional:** Tibetan and Khotanese perspectives (valuable for a secondary point-of-view character).
- **Cut:** Byzantine and Indian ends of the network.
