# 10 · Environmental footprint

- A Zurich LCA (treeze, 2018, NMC cells) found cell production causes 83 % of the battery system's
  impact. Future cells would cut it by ~75 %, and second-life cells by ~60 % [@treeze2018lca]. The embodied footprint cited for today's
  batteries is ~185 kg CO2-eq per kWh of capacity. 🔎 That figure comes from a search snippet and was not confirmed in the table.
- Comparative LCAs find chemistry and grid mix matter, and LFP avoids cobalt [@levarlet2020lca; @krebs2020iea;
  @energieschweiz-solarbatt]. Older work shows the long-standing trade-off between sizing and footprint [@celik2007lca].
- Switzerland's grid mix is already low-carbon (hydro, nuclear), so shifting your own solar
  by a few hours saves little CO2. The environmental case for a *home* battery is weak compared with
  heat pumps, EVs or more PV. This is an inference from the sources above plus the BFE system study
  [@energeiaplus2026speicher]. ⚠️
- End of life: the disposal fee is included in the price. About 70 % of metals are recovered, and nickel and cobalt up to 95 %
  [@energieschweiz-solarbatt]. Second-life products (e.g. Modual, and the Stadt Zürich bonus) reuse EV cells
  [@modual; @stadtzh2026; @swissolar2025batt].

## Purpose note

This is optional unless environmental benefit is one of your motives. If it is, spend the money on load
shifting, a heat-pump boiler or more PV first.
