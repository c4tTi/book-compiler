# 06 · Alternatives and complements (often cheaper per kWh)

Swiss guidance ranks measures roughly: **shift loads first, store heat second,
store electricity third** [@eigenverbrauch-handbuch; @hev2025speichern; @energieschweiz-speicher].

## 6.1 Load shifting (almost free)

Run the dishwasher, washing machine and dryer at midday with timers. Let the EMS or inverter
switch the boiler and heat pump on surplus via SG-Ready [@eigenverbrauch-handbuch; @swissolar-pvwp2021].
This typically takes self-consumption from 15-30 % to 40-60 % before any battery [@beobachter2023]. ✅

## 6.2 Heat as storage

- **Heat-pump water heater / boiler on PV surplus**: a heat-pump boiler uses about a third of the power of an electric
  boiler. It pays back in ~5-8 years (installer source ⚠️) [@hev2025speichern].
- **Overheating the buffer tank** (50-55 °C instead of 30-35 °C) stores ~10 kWh of heat
  [@hev2025speichern].
- EnergieSchweiz calls thermal storage "inexpensive and economically rational" [@energieschweiz-speicher].
  The BFE system study ranks thermal and large batteries above home batteries [@energeiaplus2026speicher].

## 6.3 The electric car as the battery

- An EV has 60-80 kWh, several times a home battery, and **charging it on surplus is the
  cheapest "storage"** available today [@energieschweiz-bidi].
- **V2H/V2G** (bidirectional) works technically. The Swiss V2X pilot ran 50 Honda e cars
  at 40 sites. But chargers are expensive (a new Helion unit ~CHF 3,300, others CHF 12-21k), and interoperability
  (ISO 15118-20) is expected ~2028 [@energieschweiz-bidi; @helion-bidi; @beobachter2023]. ⚠️ If you plan an
  EV within 2-3 years, consider waiting.
- Critics argue V2G revenue is eaten by battery wear [@akkudoktor2026; @akkudoktor-forum].

## 6.4 Share with neighbours (vZEV / LEG)

Since 2026, surplus can go to neighbours via vZEV or an LEG with a grid-fee discount.
For a single-family house this pays only if neighbours actually buy the power. It is
an alternative to a battery for summer surplus and not a substitute for evening supply
[@upgrid-zevleg; @bkw-zevleg; @ekz2025rueck].

## Purpose note

- **Essential:** do 6.1 and 6.2 first. They raise self-consumption for little money and
  also reduce the battery size you need.
- If an EV is coming soon, fold it into the decision. It may make a stationary battery redundant.
