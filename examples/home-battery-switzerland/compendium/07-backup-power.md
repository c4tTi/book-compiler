# 07 · Backup power during outages

- **A PV system without battery produces nothing in a blackout.** The inverter disconnects
  immediately [@swissolar-netzausfall; @se2019backup]. ✅
- Three levels [@swissolar-netzausfall; @pvforum-notstrom; @sonnen-ersatzstrom]:
  1. **Emergency socket (Notstrom):** one socket, ≤ ~3.6 kW. Possible with many AC retrofits.
     Fridge yes, stove no.
  2. **Backup (Ersatzstrom):** the whole house, or selected circuits, switches to island mode via an automatic
     transfer switch. Needs a backup-capable hybrid/all-in-one and wiring work. PV keeps charging
     the battery during the outage. Black-start capability matters.
  3. **Off-grid (Insel):** out of scope.
- Costs: backup adds ~CHF 1,500 or ~+20 % [@beobachter2023; @energieschweiz-solarbatt]. German figures:
  €300-800 for a socket, €1,000-2,500 for whole-house backup (installer blog ⚠️) [@sonnen-ersatzstrom].
- How often is it needed? Swiss outages average about 19 minutes per customer per year (2025). 🔎 That figure is second-hand
  and should be checked in ElCom statistics [@elcom-versorgung]. Winter shortage scenarios would mean planned
  rolling cuts, and a battery with little winter PV only bridges hours.

## Purpose note

Buy backup capability only if outages matter to you (home office, medical devices, freezer,
heat pump circulation). Decide it **before** choosing the product, because it rules out many AC retrofit
options.
