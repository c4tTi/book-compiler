# 10. Alternatives and stepping stones

If the site check (chapter 3), your landlord or the wild-bee argument (chapter 2) speaks against a honeybee colony on your own balcony, these options keep you close to bees.

## 10.1 Wild bees on the balcony

- The city asks residents to help wild bees with **diverse native flowering plants across the seasons, bare soil and dead wood**, and supports property owners through its green-space programme [@stzhwildbienen]. On a balcony: native perennials and herbs, sand-filled trays, stems and properly drilled hardwood blocks.
- **Mason-bee kits (BeeHome, Wildbiene + Partner, Zurich):** a nesting box plus mason-bee cocoons each spring, with the box returned for cleaning in autumn. From about CHF 98–175 (snippet) [@wbpbeehome]. A Zurich family reports it works on a 4th-floor balcony and needs almost no care [@swissfamilyfun].
  - ⚠️ **Critique:** wildbee.ch argues the scheme helps only **two common species** that need no protection, ships live insects by post, and feeds a commercial cocoon trade sold to agriculture [@wildbeech]. Critics of "insect hotels" make similar points about badly built nesting aids. Use it as a **nature-watching tool**, not as conservation.

## 10.2 Bee sponsorship (Bienenpatenschaft)

You pay a beekeeper, visit the colony and get honey:
- Zürcher Bienenfreunde teaching apiary: ~CHF 150/year (multi-year), 4 × 500 g honey [@zbfpaten].
- Wabe3 (Zurich rooftops): private sponsorships with hive visits [@wabe3paten].
- Züriblüte: ~CHF 1/day including 3 kg honey [@zueribluete].
(Prices from search snippets 🔎.)

## 10.3 Learn first, own later

- **Take the course without owning bees.** The Stadtbienen and club courses need no garden or colony [@stadtbienenkurs; @zbfkurse].
- **Keep your colonies off-site** (club, friend's garden, farm edge of town). This is a common beginner route (chapter 3.5).
- **Accompanied beekeeping** with a mentor from the club [@zbfwerden].
- The canton of Bern runs **shared teaching apiaries** as a way to manage density while training people. That is one model Zurich could copy [@stgallerbauer].

## 10.4 Natural and "wild" honeybee approaches

FreeTheBees promotes tree-cavity-like housing (Klotzbeute, SwissTree, Zeidler cavities) and natural beekeeping [@freethebees]. Seeley's research on wild colonies is the scientific background [@seeley2019]. Swiss animal-disease law (registration, treatment and reporting duties) still applies to any managed colony 🔎.

## Purpose note

- **Essential if the balcony fails the site check:** 10.3.
- **Nice to have regardless:** wild-bee planting (10.1). It is the one balcony action that every source agrees helps biodiversity.
