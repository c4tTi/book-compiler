# 8. Honey: harvest, food law, labelling

## 8.1 Own use vs giving away or selling ✅

- The Food Act (LMG) **does not apply to primary production for private household use** (Art. 2 para. 4a) [@lmrhonig2022]. Honey eaten at home: no formalities.
- **"Placing on the market"** includes any passing-on, **paid or free** (Art. 6 LMG) [@lmrhonig2022]. Jars given to neighbours or colleagues are therefore, strictly speaking, covered 🔎 (literal reading; enforcement practice for small gifts not researched).
- **Notification (Meldepflicht):** anyone handling food must notify the cantonal authority. **Occasional small-scale sales at bazaars, school fairs and the like are exempt** (LGV Art. 20) [@lmrhonig2022]. In Zurich this goes to the Kantonales Labor; registration is free (snippet) [@klzh].
- **No permit (Bewilligung) is needed** for businesses that only produce or sell bee products (LGV Art. 21 para. 2h) [@lmrhonig2022]. ⚠️ Some web summaries claim the opposite; the ordinance text is clear.
- The veterinary office controls primary production for keepers with **more than 40 colonies** [@zhbienenkonzept], which won't be you.

## 8.2 Quality rules

- Legal **maximum water content 20%** (general honey) [@lmrhonig2022]. The voluntary **Goldsiegel** (apisuisse quality seal) requires **≤18.5%**, documented practice and training, and is checked by honey controllers from the associations [@goldsiegel]. The club Grundkurs includes a voucher for a quality-seal inspection [@bsimkerwerden].
- Harvest only **capped, ripe** combs, extract hygienically, strain and store sealed. Your own Varroa treatments must follow the product rules so no residues reach the honey (course content; BGD sheets) [@bgdmerkblaetter].

## 8.3 Labelling (pre-packed jars) ✅

Mandatory, clearly legible and durable [@honigetikette]:
1. **"Honig"** (the legal name). "Rein", "naturbelassen" or "100% natürlich" are **misleading**, because honey must be pure by definition.
2. **Name and full address** of producer/bottler/seller.
3. **Country of production** (e.g. "Schweizer Honig" or "CH-8004 Zürich").
4. **Net weight**, with minimum font height (4 mm for 250 g–1 kg jars, 3 mm for 50–200 g).
5. **Best-before** ("mindestens haltbar bis Ende …"), no abbreviations.
6. **Lot number** after "L" (e.g. harvest date + site).

Optional, with conditions: blossom/forest honey, variety names (must be predominantly from that plant), geographic names (e.g. "Zürcher Stadthonig" must be only from there), nutrition table. **Health claims are forbidden** [@honigetikette]. Labels in German, French or Italian (snippet) [@klzh].

## 8.4 Urban honey quality

Studies abroad measure heavy metals and other contaminants in urban honey and pollen. No Zurich-specific honey contamination data was found in this research (gap) 🔎. The Goldsiegel control, or a lab test via the club, gives certainty.

## Purpose note

- **Essential only if you give away or sell:** 8.1 and 8.3.
- **Optional:** Goldsiegel (8.2), which is worth it if you want to sell and be recognised.
