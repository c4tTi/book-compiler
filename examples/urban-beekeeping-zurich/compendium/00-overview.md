# Urban beekeeping in Zurich: overview

*Compiled 2026-09-28 for a beginner who wants to keep honeybees on a balcony or roof in the city of Zurich. Written in English. Most sources are Swiss-German; German terms are glossed in `90-glossary.md`.*

## The short answer

**It is legal and possible, but it is not a free-for-all, and the tide in Zurich is running against new urban hives.**

1. **Legally** you need (a) **written consent** from your landlord/owner, (b) **registration** of the apiary with the cantonal Veterinäramt **within 3 working days**, with the ID sign fixed at the hive, (c) **stock and treatment records**, and (d) the duty to **report foulbrood** and respect restricted zones. No course or licence is legally required (yet) [@vetazh2022; @bfzhgesetz; @bsimkerwerden; @srf2023] ✅.
2. **The City of Zurich** has allowed **no new honeybee stands on city-owned land since 1 Sep 2025**. Private balconies and roofs are not banned, but the city no longer promotes honeybees because of competition with its ~215 wild-bee species [@stzhfaq; @stzhwildbienen] ✅.
3. **Official site advice is sceptical of roofs** (BGD: "avoid … roofs"; Geneva: strongly advises against), and balconies bring the neighbour, swarm and droppings issues closest to people [@bgd49; @gech; @bsnachbarn] ⚠️.
4. **Bee health is the real work:** a fixed Varroa plan (summer formic acid from July, a second treatment by mid-September, oxalic acid in winter), swarm checks every 7–9 days from April to July, and a watch for the **Asian hornet**, now spreading in the city [@bgd11; @stzhhornisse2026] ✅.
5. **Start with a course.** The standard is the 2-year club Grundkurs (CHF ~950). The Zürcher Bienenfreunde course for 2026/27 is **full**. Wabe3 starts 22 Nov 2026 and Stadtbienen in spring 2027 [@zbfkurse; @wabe3; @stadtbienenkurs].
6. **Budget** ~CHF 1,900–2,100 to start with one colony including the course, then ~CHF 200/yr; ~10 h per colony per year plus course time (`workbench/model.py`) [@zbfwerden; @apimat; @aumeierzeit].

## Shape of the field

The topic sits where **animal-disease law** (registration, notifiable diseases), **apiculture practice** (Swiss BGD concept, club training), **urban ecology** (honeybee vs wild-bee competition, WSL research), and **civil/tenancy law** (neighbours, landlord) meet. Around it are **food law** (honey), **allergology** (stings) and **invasion biology** (Asian hornet, small hive beetle).

The main **schools/voices**:
- the **Swiss beekeeping establishment** (BienenSchweiz, BGD, apisuisse, clubs): training, Varroa concept, honey quality; also wary of too many urban hives;
- **wild-bee conservation** (City of Zurich, WSL researchers, Bienenfachstelle, wildbee.ch): honeybees are livestock, and cities need flowers and nest sites, not more hives;
- **urban/natural beekeeping** (Wabe3, Stadtbienen, FreeTheBees): roofs are good sites, low-intervention beekeeping.

**Open questions:** whether honeybees measurably harm wild bees in Zurich (the 2023 garden study found no direct effect, while the 2022 multi-city study found forage insufficient) [@casanellesabella2023; @casanellesabella2022]; whether a mandatory beekeeping licence will come [@srf2023]; how fast the Asian hornet establishes [@stzhhornisse2026].

## Best sources by type

- **Official rules:** Kanton Zürich Veterinäramt, *Registrierungsfragen Bienenstände* (2022) [@vetazh2022]; Bienenfachstelle, *Gesetzliche Grundlagen* [@bfzhgesetz].
- **Practical guidance (the one set to print):** BGD Merkblätter, especially **1.1 Varroa concept** and **4.9 Standortwahl** [@bgd11; @bgd49], plus the index [@merkblaetterverz].
- **Neighbours:** BienenSchweiz, *Imkern in der Nachbarschaft* (2023). It is candid and short [@bsnachbarn].
- **City policy:** Stadt Zürich, *Wildbienen – Fragen und Antworten* [@stzhfaq].
- **Book:** *Das Schweizerische Bienenbuch* (5 vols.) [@bienenbuch]; in English, *The Beekeeper's Handbook* [@sammataro2011]; for delight, Seeley's *Honeybee Democracy* [@seeley2010].
- **Papers:** Casanelles-Abella & Moretti 2022 (+ correction) and Casanelles-Abella et al. 2023, the Zurich evidence [@casanellesabella2022; @casanellesabella2022corr; @casanellesabella2023]; Geldmann & González-Varo 2018 [@geldmann2018]; Rosenkranz et al. 2010 on Varroa [@rosenkranz2010].
- **Primary/historical:** HLS article on Swiss beekeeping [@hlsbienen]; Kramer's *Schweizerischer Bienenvater* (1901) [@kramer1901].
- **Courses:** Zürcher Bienenfreunde Grundkurs [@zbfkurse]; Wabe3 [@wabe3]; Stadtbienen [@stadtbienenkurs].
- **Video / audio:** Bienenfachberatung formic-acid video [@ytbinder]; *Drohnenschlacht* podcast [@drohnenschlacht].
- **Tools / data:** `workbench/model.py`; Apiara app [@apiara]; cantonal bee concept figures [@zhbienenkonzept]; winter-loss surveys [@winterverluste2023].
- **Community:** Zürcher Bienenfreunde (club, mentoring, sponsorship) [@zbfwerden]; Imkerforum threads on balcony beekeeping (German) [@imkerforumbalkon].
