# 9. Courses, clubs and contacts in Zurich

## 9.1 How training works in Switzerland ✅

- BienenSchweiz's national training concept (developed for the Federal Office for Agriculture) is built on the **Grundkurs**: **18 half-days over two years**, theory and practice, run by the regional **sections (clubs)**. The guide price is **CHF 950–1,250**, including the 5-volume *Schweizerisches Bienenbuch*, course folder, a year of the Bienen-Zeitung, a Goldsiegel inspection voucher, and a certificate [@bsimkerwerden]. **Not legally required, strongly recommended** [@bsimkerwerden].
- An **online course** from BienenSchweiz supplements (does not replace) the Grundkurs [@bsonlinekurs].
- Associations have pushed for a mandatory "beekeeping licence" [@srf2023]. If it comes, a Grundkurs will likely be the route to it 🔎.

**Language:** all courses found are in German. No English-language beekeeping course in Zurich was found (gap). Ask course leaders; many will answer questions in English 🔎.

**Which one?** If you want the **standard Swiss path** (Swiss textbook, club mentoring, a place in the local network, likely recognition for any future licence), take the **club Grundkurs**. The club is also where you'll find a site, borrow an extractor and get swarm help. **Wabe3** is closest to your "rooftop in Zurich" situation and starts soonest. **Stadtbienen** suits you if you prefer low-intervention beekeeping. You can combine a short course now with the club Grundkurs later.

## 9.3 Clubs and associations

- **Zürcher Bienenfreunde**: the BienenSchweiz section for the city of Zurich. Teaching apiary in Witikon, courses, mentoring ("begleitetes Imkern"), sponsorships. Contact kurse@zuercher-bienenfreunde.ch (from search snippet) [@zbfwerden; @zbfkurse; @zbfpaten].
- **Kantonalverband Zürcher Imkervereine**: the cantonal umbrella body with 14 sections [@zhbienenvereine].
- **BienenSchweiz** (German- and Romansh-speaking CH; ~15,000 members) under **apisuisse** [@wikiimkereich]: Bienen-Zeitung, Merkblätter, shop, marketplace, liability cover [@haftpflicht; @bsmarktplatz].
- **mellifera.ch** (dark bee breeders) [@mellifera]. **FreeTheBees** (wild-living honeybees, natural hives) [@freethebees].

## 9.5 Reading, listening, watching

- **Core textbook:** *Das Schweizerische Bienenbuch* (5 vols., BienenSchweiz). It descends from Kramer's *Schweizerischer Bienenvater* (1901, digitised on the Internet Archive) [@bienenbuch; @kramer1901].
- **English manual:** Sammataro & Avitabile, *The Beekeeper's Handbook* [@sammataro2011].
- **Biology, for enjoyment:** Tautz, *The Buzz about Bees* (German: *Phänomen Honigbiene*); Seeley, *Honeybee Democracy* (swarming) and *The Lives of Bees* (wild colonies) [@tautz2008; @seeley2010; @seeley2019].
- **Urban beekeeping as a social phenomenon:** Moore & Kosut, *Buzz* [@moore2020].
- **Podcast:** *Drohnenschlacht – zwei Imker, drei Meinungen* (German practitioners) [@drohnenschlacht].
- **Video:** Swiss Bienenfachberatung on formic acid treatment [@ytbinder].
- **Magazine:** *Schweizerische Bienen-Zeitung* (monthly, since 1878) [@sbzsommer; @bienenzeitung1869].
