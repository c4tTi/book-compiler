# Scope: Urban beekeeping in Zurich (balcony/roof)

- **Request:** i want to start beekeeping on my balcony/roof in zurich. compile everything i need to know - rules, equipment, bee health, courses, local clubs.
- **Purpose:** decision + learning: decide whether and how to start keeping honeybees on a balcony or roof in the city of Zurich, then learn to do it properly. Workbench = decision (site check, options, costs) + learning path + first-year checklist.
- **Audience:** the user, a beginner living in the city of Zurich. Probably reads English (request was in English); most primary sources are German, so the compendium is written in English and quotes German terms.
- **Depth:** practical and legally precise for Zurich/Switzerland; enough science to understand the wild-bee debate and bee health; not a full apiculture textbook.
- **Languages / regions:** English (output); sources in German (main), French (Geneva/Romandie view), English (science, books). Region: city and canton of Zurich, Swiss federal law. German (DE) practice sources used only for practical tips, flagged because German law differs.
- **Out of scope:** commercial beekeeping, queen breeding, migratory beekeeping, apitherapy, beekeeping outside Switzerland (except comparison).
- **Defaults chosen (user was away):** honeybees (Apis mellifera) are the main topic; wild-bee alternatives are included because the city actively discourages new honeybee colonies. Assumed the user rents (so landlord consent is covered); if they own, skip that step.
- **Started:** 2026-09-28

This file is a working document: edit or rewrite any part of it freely.

## Fields

- apiculture: bee biology, colony management, hive systems (Betriebsweise, Trachtlücke, Schwarmkontrolle)
- veterinary epidemiology: notifiable diseases, Varroa, Faulbrut, Sauerbrut (incl. pathogen microbiology), Tierseuchenverordnung
- animal-disease law: TSV registration, Bestandeskontrolle, restricted zones
- urban ecology: carrying capacity, floral resources, city policy
- conservation biology: wild bees, competition, Red List, forage (cantonal bee concept)
- civil law: ZGB 684 neighbour law, swarms, liability and liability insurance
- tenancy law: landlord consent, Art. 257f OR
- building law: permits for hives
- food law: LMG/LGV, honey labelling, water content
- allergology: venom allergy, anaphylaxis, desensitisation
- invasion biology: Asian hornet, small hive beetle
- education: courses, training concept
- history: Swiss beekeeping tradition
- policy: city/canton measures, proposed licence
- sociology: urban beekeeping as social movement
- conservation genetics: dark bee protection
