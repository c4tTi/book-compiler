# Open questions (what would change the decision, and how to find out)

| Question | Why it matters | How to find out |
|---|---|---|
| Does your lease or house rules mention animals? Will the landlord consent in writing? | Without consent there is a termination risk [@mieterverband] | Read the lease + Hausordnung; ask the Verwaltung in writing |
| Is the building city-owned (e.g. a municipal housing estate)? | City land: no new honeybee stands since 1 Sep 2025 [@stzhfaq] | Ask the Verwaltung / Liegenschaften Stadt Zürich |
| Does the City of Zurich or your Kreis have any distance or building rules for hives in the building zone? | Not found in this research; a neighbour complaint could trigger a building application [@bsnachbarn] | Ask the Amt für Baubewilligungen Stadt Zürich |
| Who is the current bee inspector for your district? | Needed for disease reporting; published list is from 2017 [@bieneninspektorenzh] | Call the Veterinäramt 043 259 41 41 [@vetazhform] |
| Does your private liability insurance cover hobby beekeeping? | The Bienen-Zeitung cover is only subsidiary [@haftpflicht] | Ask your insurer in writing |
| Will a beekeeping licence become mandatory? | Could require the full Grundkurs [@srf2023] | Watch BienenSchweiz/apisuisse news |
| How many hives are already within 500 m? | BGD density rule; forage competition [@bgd49; @casanellesabella2022] | Ask the club or Bienenfachstelle [@bfzh] |
| Is anyone at home or next door allergic? | Allergy risk is elevated in beekeepers' families [@muller2005] | GP/allergist; talk to neighbours |
| What exactly do Art. 700/719 ZGB say about swarms? | Your right to retrieve a swarm from a neighbour's land | Read on fedlex.admin.ch (SR 210) |
