## Recommendation (confidence: medium)

- If your goal is to **become a beekeeper**, the best path is **C: take a course, then keep your first 1–2 colonies off-site** (club, friend's garden, city edge) while you learn swarm control and Varroa management. Revisit the balcony or roof after one or two seasons, once you know your bees' temperament and your routine.
- If your building has a **flat, safe, accessible roof** with owner consent and few overlooked neighbours, **B** beats A. Mitigate wind (anchoring) and heat (shade), and plan a safe retreat path.
- Choose **A (balcony)** only if the flight path does not cross neighbours' balconies or windows within ~10 m, the landlord has agreed in writing, and nobody nearby is allergic.
- Whatever you choose, do **E** too (native flowering plants on the balcony). It is the one step every source agrees helps.
