# Learning path and first-steps timeline (from Oct 2026)

## Stage 0: decide (Oct–Nov 2026, ~5 h)
- Read compendium 00, 01, 02, 03. Fill in `site-check.md`.
- Ask your landlord/owner (template idea: location, 1–2 hives, registration, insurance, access, trial year).
- GP/allergy check if anyone in the household has allergies [@ahainsekt].
- **Book a course:**
  - Wabe3 Grundkurs, starts **22 Nov 2026** (CHF 950) [@wabe3], and/or
  - Stadtbienen Zurich, starts **spring 2027** (early bird until **2 Nov 2026**) [@stadtbienenkurs], and/or
  - put your name down **now** for the Zürcher Bienenfreunde Grundkurs **2027/28** (2026/27 is full) [@zbfkurse].
- *Ready to move on when:* you have consent (or have chosen off-site) and a course place.

## Stage 1: foundations (winter 2026/27, ~20 h)
- Join Zürcher Bienenfreunde; attend meetings; ask for a mentor ("begleitetes Imkern") [@zbfwerden].
- Read the *Schweizerisches Bienenbuch* vols. 1–2 (craft, biology) [@bienenbuch] or Sammataro [@sammataro2011]; for enjoyment, Tautz or Seeley [@tautz2008; @seeley2010].
- Print BGD Merkblätter 1.1 (Varroa) and 4.9 (site) [@bgd11; @bgd49]; skim the index [@merkblaetterverz].
- BienenSchweiz online course as a supplement [@bsonlinekurs].
- *Ready when:* you can explain the colony year, swarming and the Varroa plan in your own words.

## Stage 2: first season with someone else's bees (Mar–Oct 2027)
- Course practicals; shadow your mentor during swarm season (Apr–Jun) and the July/September treatments [@bgd11].
- Get stung a few times under supervision; watch your reaction [@muller2005].
- Decide on hive system = the one your course/club uses. Buy personal gear [@zbfwerden].
- *Ready when:* you can open a hive calmly, find eggs and queen cells, estimate mite fall and do a formic acid treatment.

## Stage 3: your own colony (earliest: summer Jungvolk late June 2027, more realistic spring 2028)
- Register the apiary **within 3 working days**, attach the sign, start stock and treatment records [@vetazh2022; @bfzhgesetz].
- Buy a healthy Swiss colony from a registered beekeeper outside restricted zones [@apimat; @bsmarktplatz; @vetazhseuchen].
- Check liability cover [@haftpflicht].
- Follow the month table (compendium ch. 6). Report hornets and foulbrood suspicions [@stzhhornisse2026; @bsfaulbrut].
- *Ready for more colonies when:* you have overwintered at least one colony and managed a swarm season without a swarm.

## Stage 4: honey and beyond (year 2+)
- Harvest, label correctly, notify the Kantonales Labor if you sell [@honigetikette; @lmrhonig2022].
- Optional: Goldsiegel [@goldsiegel]; queen rearing (Bienenbuch vol. 3).
