# 90. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ānandamaya kośa** | The "bliss sheath", the subtlest of the five kośas |
| **Annamaya kośa** | The physical body ("food sheath") |
| **Autogenic training** | Johannes Schultz's self-suggestion relaxation method (1932), grown out of hypnosis research; a Western ancestor of the body-part sequence |
| **Chidākāśa** | "Space of consciousness"; the dark space seen behind closed eyes, used as a screen for images |
| **Default mode network (DMN)** | Brain network active in mind-wandering; decouples during yoga nidra in experienced meditators (`06`) |
| **Dhāraṇā** | Concentration; the sixth limb of Patañjali's yoga |
| **Hypnagogia** | The transitional state between waking and sleep, with spontaneous imagery |
| **Inner Resource** | iRest term: a felt sense of safety/well-being to return to during the practice |
| **iRest** | Integrative Restoration, Richard Miller's clinical adaptation of yoga nidra |
| **Jāgrat** | The waking state |
| **K-complex, sleep spindle** | EEG hallmarks of stage 2 sleep; absent in the Italian yoga nidra EEG study (`06`) |
| **Kośa** | "Sheath"; one of five layers of the person (Taittirīya Upaniṣad) |
| **Manomaya kośa** | The mental/emotional sheath |
| **Nidrā** | Sleep; in the Yoga Sūtra (1.10) one of the five fluctuations of mind |
| **Nidrā-Kālarātri** | Early goddess of sleep, swoon, death and night (c. 3rd–5th c.), behind the Devī Māhātmya's Yoganidrā (`01`) |
| **NSDR** | "Non-Sleep Deep Rest", a secular label popularized around 2021 |
| **Nyāsa** | Tantric practice of placing mantras/deities on body parts; claimed source of body rotation |
| **Pratyāhāra** | Withdrawal of the senses; fifth limb of Patañjali's yoga |
| **Prāṇa** | Life energy, closely linked with breath |
| **Prāṇamaya kośa** | The energy/breath sheath |
| **Progressive (muscle) relaxation** | Edmund Jacobson's method of tensing and releasing muscle groups (1929) |
| **Relaxation response** | Herbert Benson's term (1975) for the common physiological effect of relaxation practices |
| **Rotation of consciousness** | Moving awareness quickly through body parts in a fixed order |
| **Sākṣī** | The witness; the observer of experience |
| **Samādhi** | Absorption; the eighth limb of Patañjali's yoga |
| **Saṃskāra** | Latent impression from past experience |
| **Sankalpa** | Resolve/intention; a short positive statement planted in the practice |
| **Śavāsana** | "Corpse pose"; lying on the back, the classic posture |
| **Śavayātrā** | "Inner pilgrimage" through the body, Himalayan-tradition relaxation |
| **Sleep onset (N1)** | The gradual transition from drowsy wakefulness into stage 1 and early stage 2 sleep; the physiological 'threshold' of yoga nidra |
| **Sophrology** | Alfonso Caycedo's relaxation-and-consciousness method (1960), the francophone cousin of yoga nidra (`11`) |
| **Suṣupti** | Dreamless deep sleep |
| **Svapna** | The dreaming state |
| **Theta** | EEG rhythm (4–8 Hz) of drowsiness and stage 1 sleep; the most consistent EEG change in experienced yoga nidra practitioners |
| **Tiefenentspannung** | German: 'deep relaxation', the usual German umbrella term for yoga nidra, PMR and autogenic training (`11`) |
| **Turīya** | "The fourth"; awareness underlying waking, dream and deep sleep |
| **Vijñānamaya kośa** | The wisdom/discernment sheath |
| **Vṛtti** | Fluctuation of the mind (Yoga Sūtra 1.2) |
| **Yoganidrā** | Yogic sleep; also a goddess in the Devī Māhātmya |
