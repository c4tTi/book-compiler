# 0. Overview: Yoga Nidra on One Page

## The field in brief

**Yoga nidra** ("yogic sleep") names three things that most books blur:
a very old **word** (Vishnu's cosmic sleep; a goddess of sleep in the
*Devī Māhātmya*; in medieval haṭha texts a samādhi-like state), a modern
**method** (a guided, lying-down practice systematised by Swami
Satyananda at the Bihar School of Yoga in the 1960s and published in 1976), and
a **state** (awareness held at the threshold of sleep). The method drew on
tantric *nyāsa* and on Western relaxation therapy (Jacobson, Schultz's
autogenic training, autosuggestion), and a French book (Boyes 1973) predates
Satyananda's. From Bihar it branched into clinical **iRest** (Richard
Miller; US military and VA), post-lineage **Total Yoga Nidra** (Dinsmore-Tuli
& Tuli), Amrit/I AM, the Himalayan tradition, contemporary spiritual
teachers (Stanley, Brody), and the secular **NSDR** of apps and podcasts.

**Who studies it:** yoga teachers write most of the books; Indologists and
historians of modern yoga study the word and the method's origins;
neuroscientists (PET, EEG, fMRI), sleep researchers and clinical
psychologists study its effects; nursing and integrative medicine test it in
clinics; hypnosis research and sophrology are its closest relatives; the
wellness industry sells it.

**Main open questions**
1. *Ancient or modern?* The word is ancient; the method is 20th-century (`01`, `08` §1).
2. *Does it work, and better than what?* It beats no treatment; against other
   relaxation, effects shrink to small or none. Studies are mostly small and
   weak (`06` §6.0).
3. *What is the state?* Theta-rich drowsiness in experienced practitioners,
   default-mode decoupling, no sleep spindles; the "conscious delta sleep"
   claim is untested (`03`, `06`).
4. *Is it hypnosis?* A close cousin with a different emphasis (`03` §3.12).
5. *Is it safe?* Generally, but trauma, dissociation and some psychiatric
   conditions need care; lineage abuse is documented (`07`, `08` §6).
6. *Whose practice is it?* Lineage, clinic, app or rest movement (`08` §9, `11`).

the history with scholars rather than lineage lore (`01`), state the
evidence in one honest sentence backed by 2025–26 meta-analyses (`06` §6.0),
