## In one paragraph
A short (75-page) Hindi monograph on sleep and deep sleep by a Sanskritist (catalogued as M.A., Vyakaranacharya), published in Varanasi in 1969, who later co-edited the *Encyclopedia of Indian Philosophies* volumes on Sāṃkhya and Yoga. Its catalogue subtitle (my translation) presents it as an analysis from the standpoint of *adhyātma-śāstra*, claiming a basic agreement between Western psychology and Indian spiritual science and aiming to show the depth of *yogaja-prajñā* (yoga-born insight). That is all that is known about its content; the argument itself has not been seen.
