## In one paragraph
The Scandinavian school's general beginners' manual of yoga, presented from a tantric angle as practical tools to add to an ordinary life (publisher and Weiser blurbs). It is not a yoga nidra book. Its only value for your project is **where it puts yoga nidra** in a general curriculum, plus its date. For his method, go to janakananda1997 and janakananda2021.

## Structure
Table of contents seen for the German editions only. Headings are my translations; page numbers are German.
- **2016 outline:** Part 1 introduction and background (from p. 3); Part 2 body, breath and energy (asana, breathing, cleansing, tantra, chakras; from p. 25); **Part 3 the senses and the mind (p. 96)**; Part 4 concentration and meditation, including tratak and ajapa japa (from p. 103); Part 5 yoga in daily life (from p. 119).
- **Chapter 9 (2016), on finding a relaxed relationship to influences:** how pratyahara works (97); **Yoga Nidra – deep relaxation (99)**; Inner Silence, Antar Mauna (100).
- **Chapter 9 (1991):** the use of pratyahara (97); **"The little Yoga Nidra" (98)**; Inner Silence, 4 stages (100).
- I have not seen the contents of the 1975, 1976 or 1992 editions, or the English section titles.

## Core ideas
- **Yoga as added tools, not a new lifestyle** (Weiser blurb).
- **A graded curriculum** (contents): body and breath, then the senses and mind (pratyahara), then concentration and meditation, then daily life.
- **Yoga nidra is placed as a pratyahara practice,** after body and breath work, right before Inner Silence (contents). That is the one yoga-nidra fact this book's contents establish.

## The practice as this book teaches it
**Not seen; cannot be reported.** I have no evidence on stages, posture, sankalpa, sleep instruction, length or wording. The only facts are size and position: about one to two pages in the pratyahara chapter (judged from heading start pages only). The 1991 heading "The little Yoga Nidra" shares a name with the short practice on *Experience Yoga Nidra* (janakananda1997), but whether the book prints that practice, an outline or something else is unknown. For Janakananda's actual guidance, see janakananda1997 (machine-transcribed Swedish stream of both practices) and janakananda2021.

## What's distinctive
- **Placement, not method.** Like satyananda1974 (*Meditations from the Tantras*), it puts yoga nidra immediately before antar mauna, but in a short section rather than a long chapter.
- **Date.** Swedish 1975, English 1976: an early Western publication by a direct Satyananda disciple. Whether the 1975 text already includes yoga nidra is unverified. The nidra section is not "the first Western yoga nidra chapter": in the editions seen it is a short section, and Boyes 1973 is earlier.

## Strengths and limitations
- **Strengths:** a clear placement model; illustrated self-study format; Goodreads average 4.14 from 36 ratings (all editions).
- **Limitations:** very little yoga nidra; no independent critical review found (one Goodreads reader finds it dated); several editions with different translators and a retitled nidra section, so any citation must name the edition.

## For your own book
- **Take:** only the placement model (pratyahara, then Inner Silence, after body and breath; it matches satyananda1974) and the corrected date (Swedish 1975, English 1976), naming the edition you cite. Everything else from Janakananda (short and long practices, *nyāsa*, sankalpa, "relaxed confrontation" from the school's articles) belongs to janakananda1997 and janakananda2021, not this book.
- **Skip or treat critically:** don't use this book as a source for the method; don't repeat the claim that it is "the first Western book with a complete yoga nidra chapter" (unsupported, see above).
